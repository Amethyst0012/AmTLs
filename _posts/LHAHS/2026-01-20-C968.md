The exam led by the students was suspended.

However, there were no significant issues.

In fact, they gained something even greater than the results they had prepared for the exam.

"From the beginning, the main goal of this exam was to narrow the gap between hero candidates and shadow candidates."

Carl said this as he read the reports submitted by the first-year students.

"Don't you think we got better data than expected? At this rate, we might see new party lineups."

In response to Carl's question, Chloe nodded.

"Yeah. The way the first-years from the shadows increased party stability was impressive."

The missions of the Shadows are largely twofold.

The first is the elimination of traitors.

The second is protecting heroes from those traitors.

Although the task of eliminating traitors is so impactful that it overshadows the rest, protecting heroes is also a specialized role of the Shadows.

And this time, the first-year students from the Shadows made their presence known.

Miraculously, not a single person died during the mission.

Even with Great Heroes and Dawn heroes on their side, this was nothing short of a miracle.

One of the reasons this was possible was because of those first-year students from the Shadows.

Of course, if they had been merely pure Shadows, such noticeable results wouldn't have emerged.

"Studying heroes as hero candidates probably made them more effective, don't you think?"

"Most likely."

Chloe's question was met with a nod from Chen Xia.

Since long ago, shadows have stood behind heroes.

But never before had they enhanced each other's abilities like this.

It was a result made possible only because they studied and understood heroes before becoming Shadows.

"There are a few Shadows in the third years like me and among the second years too, but I never expected such visible efficiency."

Chen Xia muttered in surprise, and Chloe responded.

"The conditions were limited before. But this time, we had many shadow candidates, and they came from various races too."

Chloe smiled softly.

"It's a possibility we could only discover because the era of dividing heroes and shadows has ended."

"That's true."

The roles of heroes and shadows are ultimately distinct.

That means the path chosen must align with one's aptitude.

However, even among those who walk the path of shadows, there are those who dream of becoming heroes.

Like Chen Xia, who possesses the qualities of both a shadow and a hero.

But...

*'Perhaps there was never a need to separate them in the first place.'*

As the hidden history of the Shadows came to light, there was confusion among them.

Some, claiming they were no different from heroes, tried to walk toward the light.

Others, loyal to their convictions, chose to remain in darkness. 

Among them were those who quietly made their choices, while others passionately argued for their paths.

"In the end, there was no right answer."

Each person's chosen path ultimately becomes their answer.

The same applies to Chen Xia.

She engraved her name in the Hero Record, obtaining the title of Hero—something she had long dreamed of since childhood.

A hero who lives in radiant light, bringing hope to many.

However, at least for now, Chen Xia had no intention of living in that light.

*'Because Leo-doryeong is there.'*

The one who brought light into her life.

Until the day he lays down his burdens.

Or perhaps, she might live in his shadow for the rest of her life.

If that day comes, will she regret it?

*'No. Because it's the path I chose.'*

What she believed was right.

All she had to do was pursue that with conviction.

It's simple, yet surprisingly difficult to realize.

Chen Xia's expression lightened with relief.

This event will mark a great transformation for both heroes and shadows.

And this transformation will certainly bring about positive change.

*'No. I'll make sure it does.'*

As the new Emperor of Xian.

As the next king of the shadows.

*'I have a new mission now.'*

Chen Xia lightly swung her legs and hummed a tune.

It wouldn't be easy, but strangely, having a purpose filled her with joy.

At that moment.

A commotion was heard from outside.

The excited first-years were chatting about the recent events.

"Ugh... I'm jealous. Everything's basically over now. Can't I do it later?"

At Carl's question, Chloe smiled.

"Nope."

"Come on! I joined midway! Why do I have to finish everything?"

"Well, Carl, you're the official slave of the student council until graduation."

The tent flap opened and Elena entered.

Floating plates of food surrounded her.

"Eat while you work."

"Elena-sunbae. Aren't you working?"

"I'm graduating soon, you know? It's time to start passing things on. And fortunately, we have an excellent vice president candidate right here."

Elena placed a hand on Chloe's shoulder from behind.

"With such a capable junior, this sunbae feels secure."

"So, you're not handing it over to Lyl-sunbae after all?"

"Well, I wouldn't mind handing it to Lyl."

Elena shrugged at Carl's comment.

Just as Carl raised an eyebrow in surprise—

"Of course, if Lyl becomes vice president, you'll have a harder time, Carl."

"I've always supported Chloe."

Carl declared firmly.

Listening to this, Chloe asked, "By the way, Elena-sunbae. Why is Xia helping us with our work?"

Currently, Chen Xia, Chloe, and Carl were drafting reports to submit to the professors after the conquest of the Hero's World.

Since it was based on the reports written by the Lumene first-years, the workload was significant.

Originally, this should have been the job of the student council president and vice president.

Carl was acting student council president.

Chloe, as the vice president candidate, was substituting for Elena.

But Chen Xia wasn't either.

"Don't tell me Xia is also a vice president candidate?"

Chloe asked, and Elena smiled.

"Xia is the president's secretary."

"What?"

"Usually the vice president also acts as the president's secretary. But our president is always busy with external matters, you know? Even right now."

Mentioning the currently absent Leo, Elena moved behind Chen Xia.

"So we decided to make Xia the president's secretary. You two support Leo well together."

"There's also the intention of not entrusting all the fish to one cat, right?"

"Exactly. Keep each other in check."
