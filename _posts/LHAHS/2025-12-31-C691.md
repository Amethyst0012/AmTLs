"I see. So the dispelling magic doesn't work?"

Giath stroked his chin.

The magic Leo had just used was a spell Giath was also familiar with.

Back in the Age of Calamity.

It was a spell that had hunted countless giant demons.

It was the unique magic of the Hero of the Beginning, Kyle.

The giant demons, with their massive builds, possessed weight as overwhelming as their size.

The heavier something is, the more it is affected by gravity.

Kyle took advantage of this, using gravity magic to hunt giants.

But it wasn't simply a spell that multiplied gravity.

Tartaros, too, knew of this weakness and had prepared countermeasures. When a giant demon is subjected to gravity beyond what its body can endure, the Blessing of Erebos is activated.

The blessing of the Flame of Calamity bestowed upon the giants.

Because of this, gravity magic powerful enough to destroy the body normally wouldn't work.

However, the reason Kyle could still hunt giants with his magic was because his mana attribute destroyed Erebos's blessing.

Of course, it wasn't magic that worked on all giant demons.

Some could endure the gravity with strong dark mana.

Others adept in dark magic could respond with Dispel.

But if they lacked such defenses, they had no choice but to succumb.

That was why Giath spent years researching Kyle's magic.

But Kyle, after being revived in the present world, had steadily developed his magic further.

That's why Giath's dispel spell didn't work.

Of course, that didn't mean Giath's research was worthless.

The foundation of Leo's spell was what Giath already knew.

In that case, all he had to do was recalibrate the dispel code to match.

*'Besides, it's impossible to keep using large-scale magic like that repeatedly.'*

Unless you're the Poet of Star?

Even the Hero of the Beginning has limits.

*'Especially if he hasn't regained his former power yet.'*

Giath's eyes gleamed.

He continued to observe the battlefield from the rear.

Giant King Giath.

He was a cautious demon.

The complete opposite of the warlike Monster Queen, Sillatna.

At a glance, he seemed similar to the Lich King who enjoyed crushing enemies with traps and schemes, but he was different.

Giath would never enter a fight unless he was absolutely certain.

He simply observed the situation.

On this battlefield, Giath was confident he could defeat the Hero of the Beginning and Dweno.

But that didn't mean he would recklessly charge in.

The opponent was no less than the Great Hero.

One who killed a god and thwarted the ambitions of the demons.

One who turned countless impossibilities into reality.

Even if it was just two of them, utmost caution was required.

Moreover—

*'Where is Dweno?'*

The most troublesome enemy, Dweno, was nowhere to be seen.

*'What are you scheming, Hero of the Beginning... Divine Blacksmith...'*

There was definitely a scheme.

It was highly likely to be a trap targeting him.

He was surely hiding somewhere, waiting for the right moment to strike.

*'Do you think I'd fall for such a shallow trick?'*

The army could be rebuilt if lost.

But if he, the legion commander, fell—then it was all over.

Weaken the prey.

And in the moment they feel assured of victory—

*'I will sever your heads!'*

Giath's eyes flashed again.
