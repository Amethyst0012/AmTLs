A chilling silence fell.

The Lumene students looked at Leo with puzzled expressions, while the Seiren students glared at him with stiff faces.

“Wrong… interpretation? What do you mean by that, Student Leo Plov?”

Herdium asked with a smiling face.

But in his eyes lingered a cold gleam.

Seeing that, Leo scratched his cheek and said,

“That spell won’t activate.”

“Of course. This is an incomplete spell—it can’t activate in the first place.”

“Huh?”

Leo was taken aback.

And then he realized the looks the Seiren students—including Herdium—were giving him weren’t kind.

*‘What would a kid whose specialties are Aura and Summoning even know about magic?’*

Lunia, too, stared at Leo with an incredulous face.

“Ha! Acting all high and mighty just because you can use something resembling Star Sorcery! This spell is the legacy of the Poet that no one could interpret! Countless elven mages worked to restore it into this current form—how dare you call it wrong after looking at it?”

The elf boy whom Leo had beaten before snapped and shouted.

*‘Ah… so this was a lost spell?’*

Leo realized his mistake and made an awkward expression.

Meanwhile, Lunia looked startled by the boy’s words.

*‘Wait? Magic? But Leo clearly used Aura and Spiritual Power?’*

“It seems Student Leo has some knowledge of Star Sorcery. Interesting. I wonder how Star Sorcery appears through the eyes of a human.”

Herdium extended a piece of chalk with his cold voice.

“If our elves’ interpretation is wrong, will you show us yours?”

*‘Tch. Looks like I can’t avoid this.’*

It hadn’t crossed his mind that this would be a basic spell of Star Sorcery that still remained uninterpreted.

*‘Well… I guess it makes sense. This spell was created after Luna established a magic system out of Star Sorcery.’*

The Star Sorcery used by elves in this era was different at its root from what Luna had wielded.

That was only natural, since the one who had truly inherited the full essence of Star Sorcery had not been the elves, but Kyle.

*‘I died before I could pass that essence on to other elves.’*

It was no exaggeration to say that the authentic essence of Star Sorcery had been cut off with Kyle.

The Star Sorcery elves currently used was nothing more than a copy reconstructed from the formulas Velkia—Luna’s disciple—remembered, along with the grimoires Luna left behind.

*‘Even so, being able to reconstruct this much out of something incomplete is impressive.’*

Given that, it wasn’t strange that they wouldn’t know a spell considered “basic” from Luna or Kyle’s perspective.

For the elves, it was a completely lost spell.

Chastising himself for the blunder, Leo stood and walked to the chalkboard.

The Lumene students who saw that nearly fainted.

*‘Ugh! Leo-oppa, why would you do that!’*

*‘If the grade’s representative gets humiliated, it’ll be a disaster!’*

*‘Ack! Professor Harrid’s eyes are shooting lasers!’*

Even Harrid, watching from behind, was glaring at Leo with a terrifying look.

That was how outrageous Leo’s action was.

Class 5 students held their heads in agony, while the Seiren students stared at Leo as if to say, *Let’s see how good you really are.*

Herdium folded his arms, stepped back, and watched as Leo began his interpretation.

The faint sound of chalk filled the room.

Tak—!

Leo began filling in the missing parts of the broken formula.

Naturally, in that process, he used formulas of Star Sorcery that the elves no longer knew.

*‘What kind of interpretation is that?’*

*‘It looks like Star Sorcery, but I’ve never seen that formula before.’*

*‘That’s a totally weird interpretation, isn’t it?’*

The Seiren students looked puzzled at Leo’s work.

Even Lunia—renowned for her magical prowess—wore a bewildered expression.

*‘Does such a formula even exist?’*

Not one Seiren student understood how important the formulas Leo was writing on the board really were.

Instead, their heads were spinning at the increasingly complex formulas.

Only one person—Herdium—was trembling with awe as he stared at the board.

Tak—!

At last, Leo set down the chalk and stepped back.

“I’m done.”

Harrid, arms folded, stared intently at Leo.

He didn’t know enough about Star Sorcery to evaluate Leo’s formula.

Taking his eyes off Leo, Harrid looked sideways.

And then he was surprised by what he saw in Herdium’s face.

Herdium’s body was trembling.

This spell of the Poet, countless elven mages had attempted to interpret it for thousands of years and failed.

It had been deemed incomplete.

But Herdium, who had devoted his life to researching it, understood Leo’s formula perfectly.

So he knew.

*‘The spell has been fully unraveled!’*

{bl}

“Student Leo Plov. How on earth did you do it?”

Herdium immediately dragged Leo and Harrid into a small room adjoining the Grand Banquet Hall and questioned him.

Harrid, too, was staring at Leo in shock.

Under their gaze, Leo pondered briefly what he should say.

*‘The best choice is to just say I interpreted it normally.’*

He could hardly say things like *I’m Kyle,* or *I learned magic directly from Luna.* Even if he did, no one would believe him, and he shouldn’t say such things anyway.

“I’ve always had a strong interest in Star Sorcery, so I’ve studied it deeply. That particular spell—I’ve researched it for a long time.”

“That’s not a spell you could interpret just because you’re interested in Star Sorcery.”

Herdium adjusted his glasses and pressed sharply.

It was Harrid who answered instead.

“Leo Plov isn’t just a student with some knowledge of Star Sorcery.”

“Then what is he?”

“This student is an actual user of Star Sorcery.”

Herdium’s face showed astonishment at Harrid’s words.

“Star Sorcery? A human? Ah, no, it’s not that other races can’t use it, but… at such a young age?”

Star Sorcery was a system of magic created by Luna.

Over time, it had been adapted specifically for elves, so compared to other magic systems, it was particularly difficult for other races to wield.

Even if one could use it, a high level of magical proficiency was required, making it difficult to use at a young age, and even then its power was halved.

*‘But this boy—at just fifteen, in a human body—uses Star Sorcery?’*

“But even if he is a user of Star Sorcery, that doesn’t explain how he could interpret this… Can you explain the process?”

At Herdium’s question, Leo began writing out the interpretation process on paper.

As Herdium watched, his eyes filled more and more with shock.

‘If the Poet herself had interpreted it, wouldn’t it look just like this?’

That was how natural—and perfect—Leo’s interpretation was.

When Leo finally finished, Herdium took the paper and trembled.

“Leo Plov… what exactly is this spell? Have you ever used it before?”

*‘I haven’t personally used it, but…’*

Leo scratched his cheek and said,

“It’s a spell that makes flowers bloom.”

“Flowers?”

Harrid and Herdium both looked puzzled.

“Yes.”

“A-astonishing. To think that this spell, from which countless other elemental spells derived, was simply a spell to bloom flowers.”

Stunned by the Poet’s whimsicalness, Herdium muttered in disbelief.

Leo then demonstrated the magic.

A gentle light bloomed in his palm, and before long, a single flower blossomed in his hand.

“It isn’t merely a spell to make flowers bloom. During the Age of Calamity, flowers were nearly extinct.”

Herdium gazed at the flower in Leo’s hand.

“To make a flower bloom requires fire, wind, water, earth… and light.”

Leo smiled faintly at the flower in his palm.

“Perhaps, to the Poet of the Star, flowers meant something truly special.”

Looking at the flower in his hand, Leo recalled the past.

He had learned it half-forced by Luna, but never once had a reason to use it.

*‘And now I use it in this life.’*

Luna’s most beloved and cherished spell.

A basic spell, yet imbued with the very essence of all the magic she had built up—this spell to bloom flowers.

*‘If the elves knew that Luna created Star Sorcery for the sake of perfecting this spell… what kind of faces would they make?’*

As he recalled Luna’s radiant smile, Leo was lost in memories.

“Indeed! Perhaps this spell contains Luna’s profound love for nature.”

*‘No, she just liked flowers, that’s all.’*

Watching Herdium interpret it however he pleased, Leo shook his head.

“In any case, Student Leo, I should speak with Professor Harrid privately. Please spend the time in self-study like the other students.”

“Yes.”

*‘Whew, got through that without trouble.’*

Relieved inside, Leo left the room.

“Our student behaved rudely. I apologize.”

“Not at all, Professor Harrid! In fact, we should be thanking you! To think that the spell we thought incomplete from the Poet was actually a complete spell! This is a great discovery!”

Seeing Herdium’s pure delight, Harrid gave a bitter smile.

“So, this might be a strange question, but…”

“…?”

“Does Student Leo Plov perhaps have elven blood among his ancestors?”

“In Lumene, we don’t really concern ourselves with who our ancestors were, so I can’t say.”

“I see.”

“Why do you ask?”

“Well… given that Leo Plov is Lumene’s grade representative, it may be presumptuous of me to say this, but…”

“…?”

“Would you consider transferring Student Leo Plov to our academy?”

“……”

At that unprecedented transfer proposal, Harrid could only stare at Herdium with a dumbfounded face.
