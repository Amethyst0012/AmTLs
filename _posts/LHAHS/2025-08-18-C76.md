The standard of magical ability had been raised across the board.

When anyone could easily wield high-level spells, the countermeasures also became easier.

In the current era, magic wasn’t just about collecting spell counts—it required personal magical research to complicate the spell systems.

But Rauta, who only focused on memorizing powerful spells gained from another’s power instead of his own, was lacking in that regard.

Even though he could use high-level spells, they were nothing more than what he had learned through the Seirun curriculum—and that was a weakness Lunia could exploit.

Of course, preemptively dispelling spells that even she herself could not cast was by no means an easy feat.

It was an ability born from Lunia’s own talent and hard work.

“Don’t get cocky!”

*‘If I just dispel it before the spell is completed, that’s the end of it.’*

Because she was a descendant of Velkia.
