The moment Leo realized he had entered the Hero's World, his eyes snapped open.

Leo looked around.

*'A makeshift tent.'*

The place where Leo had opened his eyes was inside a tent typically used in the battlefield.

Leo got up from the bedding on one side of the fairly spacious tent and walked over to a small mirror nearby.

Snow-white hair and crimson eyes.

*'This isn't a possession-type world. No, would that even apply the same way here in the first place?'*

His face tightened as he recalled the message he had just seen.

*'The Legion Record, and the world of Sillatna.'*

Anyone could tell what that meant.

*'Those Tartaros bastards had a Record System too.'*

The Hero Record had once saved the world when it awakened.

But if Tartaros also possessed something like it too?

*'Not only they could inherit the strength of powerful old demons, but also the power of Legion Commanders.'*

The fragile balance that had barely held could collapse in an instant.

Leo's mind began to race.

*'Could it be such relic existed long ago? No, that impossible. If such power existed from the start, the Lich King wouldn't have struggled so much to raise new commanders.'*

Leo ponder.

*'The Lich King possesses a fragment of Erebos. Maybe he use the fragment's power to create this...?'*

*'No. Even Erebos wouldn’t be able to create something like this.'*

The gods referred the Hero Record as the the Record System.

Erebos was an existence on par with the gods.

In that case, it wasn't strange for him to possess knowledge about the Record System.

But possessing knowledge and actual implementation were two very different things.

The Hero Records are the very essence of the gods, created by pouring in all of their divine power.

*'No matter how powerful he is, it's impossible for him alone to recreate something made by the combined power of all the gods. Besides, the Lich King only has a fragment of Erebos' power.'*

With that in mind, Leo arrived at one guess.

*'Did they modify an existing Hero Record?'*

The Hero Record had been tainted during the process of sealing Erebos' fragment.

Although many Hero's worlds remained untouched, some had been corrupted by Erebos' power.

This page was likely one of them.

Leo organized the information he had gained from the message.

*'The ruler of the world is Sillatna. The time is the prologue. The location: Forest of Monsters.'*

The Forest of Monsters was originally the land of the elves.

And it was also Luna's homeland.

When it comes to Sillatna and the Forest of Monsters, only one person came to mind.

"Luna."

**[System Error: An unauthorized entity has invaded the world.]**

A new message appeared before his eyes.

*'Unauthorized entity? Are they talking about me?'*

Leo pondered.

"If this world is implemented the same way as the Hero Records... I should have entered it as a demon, beast, or monster from Tartaros."

Leo glanced around the tent.

What surrounded him were items used by elves from 5,000 years ago.

To anyone, Leo appeared to have entered the hero world as Luna, as usual.

*Fsshh—*

Leo stepped outside the tent.

The ash-gray sky, symbolizing the Age of Calamity, loomed overhead.

Elves bustled about in large numbers around him.

Leo muttered as he took in the sight.

*'As I thought, this was originally Luna's world. So, what I supposed to do then? Prevent the Tartaros bastards from conquering this world?'*

"Ah, you're awake, Lady Luna!"

"Luna?"

An elf youth, holding a spear in one hand and a shining shield in the other, called out to Luna.

Leo instinctively looked around searching for Luna.

In the hero's world, Luna retained memories from the past world.

Would the Luna of this world remember what had happened outside?

*'What should I say to her?'*

Leo recalled the moment he had parted ways with Luna.

At that time, Luna wasn't a being manifested through the Hero Record, but a spirit.

*'Would she remember that?'*

Just as Leo's eyes wavered slightly, the elf youth who had called out to Luna approached.

"Lady Luna! There's movement from the Monster Queen in the heart of the forest!"

Leo paused at those words.

"Luna?"

"Yes?"

"You're calling me Luna?"

"......"

The elf youth covered his face with his hand at Leo's question.

"Lady Luna, today is the crucial battle that could determine the reclamation of the forest! Yet you were drinking heavily even until the night before the battle?"

Seeing the elf youth's tearful expression, Leo glanced at the shield in his hand and saw his reflection.

No matter how he looked, it was the face of Leo Plov.

"What's going on?"

"Lord Akint... it about Lady Luna..."

At that moment, a voice was heard.

Leo turned toward it.

A familiar figure was approaching.

Akint Lubecia.

Luna's magic teacher and benefactor.

He was also the one Leo had possessed in Luna's world.

"Luna, have you been drinking again?"

Akint asked with a deep sigh.

Seeing this, Leo thought:

"Looks like... people in this world see me as Luna."

And then Leo understood the exact timing of this world.

"Just before Luna joins the subjugation force."

The time when Leo and Lysinas had come to recruit Luna after defeating Jörmungandr.

When the elves united to reclaim their lost homeland and marched into the Forest of Monsters.

"The elves lost this battle."

Leo looked at Akint.

"And this man died here."
