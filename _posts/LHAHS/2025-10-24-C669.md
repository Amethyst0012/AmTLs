Aru, still curled up in her sleeping bag, began sniffing instinctively.

A moment later—

Her eyes snapped open as she let out a wild scream and sprang up.

"Wuyaaaaah!"

Still yelling, Aru wriggled to get out of the sleeping bag.

"What's going on?" Eliana, who had been sleeping next to her, asked groggily.

That's why Aru was desperate for a proper meal.

Eliana burrowed deeper into her sleeping bag.

The other girls also didn't seem inclined to move.

While all the hero candidates had strong stamina and mental resilience, a couple of sleepless nights hadn't exhausted them.

Still, the natural human desire to sleep well when possible was irresistible.
